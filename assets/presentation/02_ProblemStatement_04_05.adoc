== From bytes per call to GC runs and spikes

[.lead]
GC runs are measured (G1, 2 GB heap); rates and spikes are derived.

[cols="3,2,3,3,3",options="header",frame=none,grid=rows]
|===
| Element 500 | B per call | GC runs per 1 B calls | MB/s at 10 M calls/s | 10 s spike
| JDK `anyMatch` | 176 | 77 | 1,760 | 17.6 GB
| JDK `findFirst` | 216 | 141 | 2,160 | 21.6 GB
| JDK `count` | 224 | 199 | 2,240 | 22.4 GB
| EC, every version | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0
|===

[.notes]
--
Rate = bytes per call × calls per second; spike = rate × 10 s, the young-generation space the spike needs or the
collections it causes. GC runs per billion calls are coarse: at element 500 a 40 s measurement saw only a few GCs.
`gc.alloc.rate` (MB/s) in JMH scales with how fast the benchmark runs; size heaps with bytes per call.
--
