== Choosing a `jstat` option

[cols="4,9",options="header",frame=none,grid=rows]
|===
| Option | Shows
| `-gcutil` | Percent used per generation, GC counts and times
| `-gc` | The same in KB: capacities and used sizes
| `-gccapacity` | Minimum, maximum and current capacity per generation
| `-gcnew` | Young generation: survivor spaces and tenuring
| `-class` | Classes loaded and unloaded
|===
