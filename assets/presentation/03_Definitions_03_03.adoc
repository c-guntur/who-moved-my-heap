== Half the size per reference

image::diagram-03-compressed-oops.svg[8-byte vs 4-byte references,role=diagram]

* Every reference field and `Object[]` slot shrinks from 8 to 4 bytes.
* Example: an `ArrayList` of 1M elements needs a 4 MB array, not 8 MB.

[.notes]
--
All movers numbers (JOL) were taken with compressed oops on (2 GB heap). Above 32 GB of heap
references double in size.
--
