[background-color="#F5F7FA"]
== Why the volatile part is the risk

[.lead]
Stream garbage grows with calls per second, not with the number of elements.

* One JDK `anyMatch` call leaves 176 B, wherever the match is.
* At 10 M calls/s that is 1.76 GB/s of young-generation garbage.
* A 10 s spike at that rate allocates about 17.6 GB.
* The same question as an Eclipse Collections loop allocates 0 B.

[.notes]
--
176 B per call is measured (shakers run 20261015-0406, `jdkAnyMatch`, match at element 500, compact headers on).
The rates are derived, not measured at that load: bytes per call × calls per second, and × 10 s for the spike.
With a 512 MB young generation, 1.76 GB/s is about 206 young collections a minute (derived).
--
