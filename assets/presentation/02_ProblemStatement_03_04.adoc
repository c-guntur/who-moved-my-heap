== Compact headers shrink nodes, not boxes

[.lead]
`-XX:+UseCompactObjectHeaders` saves 4 B per header, but objects round up to 8 B.

[cols="5,2,2,2",options="header",frame=none,grid=rows]
|===
| Retained size | Headers off | Headers on | Saved
| `HashMap<Integer, Integer>`, 1 M entries | 56.4 MB | 48.4 MB | 14%
| `HashSet<Integer>`, 1 M elements | 56.4 MB | 48.4 MB | 14%
| `ArrayList<Integer>`, 1 M, presized | 20.0 MB | 20.0 MB | 0%
| `Stream.filter().map().mapToLong()` | 304 B | 272 B | 11%
| one `Integer` | 16 B | 16 B | 0%
|===

[.notes]
--
An `Integer` is 12 B header + 4 B `int` = 16 B without compact headers, and 8 B + 4 B padded to 16 B with them.
A `HashMap$Node` goes from 32 B to 24 B, which is the whole 8 MB saved on the maps and sets.
--
