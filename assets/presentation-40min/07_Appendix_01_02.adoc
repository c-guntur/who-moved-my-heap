== `jstat`: GC statistics over time

[.track]
[.on]#Appendix#

[.lead]
`jstat` samples the GC counters of a running JVM at an interval, without a dump.

[source,text]
----
jstat -gcutil <pid> 1s 5          # five samples, one per second

    E      O      M    YGC     YGCT    FGC    FGCT     GCT      (columns trimmed)
 0.00  26.38  97.46      0    0.000      2   0.045   0.045
----

* `E`, `O`, `M`: percent used of eden, old generation and metaspace.
* `YGC` and `FGC` count young and full GCs; `GCT` is the total GC time in seconds.

[.notes]
--
Less garbage per call means fewer young GCs per second, so `YGC` grows more slowly: the benchmark
result, watched live. Values shown come from an idle JDK 25 JVM.
--
