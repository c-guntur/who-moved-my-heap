== Problem statement

[.track]
Intro · [.on]#Problem# · Definitions · Solutions · AI skills · Wrap-up

[.lead]
JDK collections and streams cost heap twice: in what they hold and in what they drop.

* Baseline: boxes and nodes make 1 M ``int``s take 20 to 48 MB.
* Volatile: 160 to 224 B of pipeline garbage on every stream call.
* Neither shows in the code, and escape analysis does not remove the stream's.
* Goal: smaller footprint and less garbage, so a spike finds free heap.
