== Problem statement

[.track]
Intro · [.on]#Problem# · Definitions · Solutions · AI skills · Wrap-up

[.lead]
JDK Collections and Stream impact the heap.

* *Baseline*: Collections have Boxed primitives / extra classes like `HashMap$Node`.
* *Volatile*: Streams produce additional 160 B - 224 B of pipeline garbage on every stream call.
* *Volatile*: Streams do not benefit from *Escape Analysis*. +
&nbsp;
* *Statement*: Larger baselines, increased volatility risk instability if there are spikes.
