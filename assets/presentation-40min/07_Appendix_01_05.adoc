== JOL: the footprint of a small list

[.track]
[.on]#Appendix#

[source,java]
----
List<Integer> list = new ArrayList<>(List.of(500, 501, 502));
System.out.println(GraphLayout.parseInstance(list).toFootprint());
----

[cols="5,2,3,3",options="header",frame=none,grid=rows]
|===
| Object | Count | Headers off | Headers on
| `Object[]` (the backing array) | 1 | 32 B | 24 B
| `Integer` | 3 | 48 B | 48 B
| `ArrayList` | 1 | 24 B | 24 B
| Total | 5 | 104 B | 96 B
|===

* `GraphLayout` follows every reference, so the boxes count too: half the bytes are `Integer`.

[.notes]
--
Real `toFootprint()` output of jol-core 0.17 on JDK 25, run with `-XX:-UseCompactObjectHeaders` and then
`-XX:+UseCompactObjectHeaders`. Only the array shrinks: 16 B header plus three 4 B references is 28 B, padded
to 32 B; with compact headers 12 B plus 12 B is exactly 24 B. The movers measure whole collections this way.
--
