== `jstat`: is the JVM busy collecting?

[.track]
[.on]#Intro# · Problem · Definitions · Solutions · AI skills · Wrap-up

[.lead]
`jstat` samples the GC counters of a running JVM at an interval, without stopping it.

[source,text]
----
$ jstat -gcutil <pid> 1s 5               # five samples, one per second
     E      O    YGC     YGCT    FGC      GCT      (columns trimmed)
 32.79   2.42      7    0.078      0    0.078
  0.00   2.42      9    0.090      0    0.090
 65.57   2.42     10    0.095      0    0.095
 28.69   2.42     12    0.107      0    0.107
 95.08   2.42     13    0.113      0    0.113
----

* `E` and `O`: percent used of eden and old generation; eden fills up and empties again.
* `YGC` counts young GCs: here about 1.5 per second, all of them for garbage.

[.notes]
--
⏱ 04:45, stay about 30 s. Intro: 02:00 to 07:00 (5 min).

Output from a small program that builds and drops a 1,000-element `ArrayList<Integer>` in a loop, on JDK 25
with G1 and `-Xmx256m`; the old generation hardly moves. Less garbage per call means `YGC` grows more slowly:
the benchmark result, watched live. `jstat -gc`, `-gccause` and the option table are in the appendix.
