== Demo: down to the basement

[.track]
[.on]#Intro# · Problem · Definitions · Solutions · AI skills · Wrap-up

[.lead]
Find a running JVM and look at what it holds.

[source,shell]
----
jps -l                                          # which JVM is that?
jcmd <pid> GC.class_histogram | head -12        # what is on its heap
jcmd <pid> VM.flags -all | grep UseCompactObjectHeaders
----

* Look for `Integer`, `HashMap$Node`, `Object[]` and `$$Lambda` near the top.
