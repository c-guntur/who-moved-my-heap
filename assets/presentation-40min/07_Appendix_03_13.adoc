== What is escape analysis?

[.track]
[.on]#Appendix#

[.lead]
A C2 JIT check: after inlining, can anyone outside the method see the new object?

* *No*: the allocation is removed; its fields become locals (scalar replacement).
* *Yes*: it is allocated on the heap as usual.
* On by default; `-XX:-DoEscapeAnalysis` turns it off.
