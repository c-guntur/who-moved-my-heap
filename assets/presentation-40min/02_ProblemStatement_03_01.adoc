== Boxing is a big cost

[.track]
Intro · [.on]#Problem# · Definitions · Solutions · AI skills · Wrap-up

[.lead]
An `Integer` takes 16 B to hold a 4 B `int`, plus a 4 B reference to it.

[cols="5,2,2",options="header",frame=none,grid=rows]
|===
| 1,000,000 elements, compact headers on | Retained | Per element
| JDK `ArrayList<Integer>`, presized | 20.0 MB | 20 B
| EC `IntArrayList` (primitive) | 4.0 MB | 4 B
| JDK `HashSet<Integer>` | 48.4 MB | 48 B
| EC `IntHashSet` (primitive) | 8.4 MB | 8 B
| JDK `HashMap<Integer, Integer>` | 48.4 MB | 48 B
| EC `IntIntHashMap` (primitive) | 16.8 MB | 17 B
|===

[.notes]

In the maps key and value are the same `Integer`, so each map holds one box per entry.
Values up to 127 come from the `Integer` cache: under 0.13% of the boxes here.
