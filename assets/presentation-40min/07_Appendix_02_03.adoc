== Growing a collection leaves slack and garbage

[.track]
[.on]#Appendix#

[.lead]
A list grown from empty is bigger than a presized one, and it dropped arrays on the way.

* `ArrayList` grows by 1.5x: up to 7% unused capacity in this run.
* Every array it outgrew became garbage; JOL only sees the final list.
* `HashMap` tables double: 46 to 50.5 B per entry, depending on n.
* Presize when the size is known: `new ArrayList<>(n)`, `HashMap.newHashMap(n)`.

[.notes]
--
`ArrayList`, 1,000,000 elements: 20,861,992 B grown against 20,000,040 B presized (4.3%); 7.0% at 400k, 600k and 900k.
EC `FastList` reached 9.8% and `IntArrayList` 48.9%: they grow in bigger steps.
The dropped arrays are inferred from the growth policy; JOL does not measure them.
`HashMap.newHashMap(n)` (Java 19 and later) was not measured in this run.
--
