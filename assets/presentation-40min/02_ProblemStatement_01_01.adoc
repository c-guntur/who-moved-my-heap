== The problem in one slide

[.track]
Intro · [.on]#Problem# · Definitions · Solutions · AI skills · Wrap-up

[.lead]
JDK Collections and `Stream` create objects on the heap that a developer may not know about.

* Boxed primitives and per-entry nodes inflate what a collection holds.
* Every stream call builds a pipeline of objects and drops it after.
* Amount of garbage produced depends on the effectiveness of the JVM's *Escape Analysis*.
* *Capturing lambdas* have potential to leave garbage on the heap.
