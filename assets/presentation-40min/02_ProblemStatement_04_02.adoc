== Where the bytes come from

[.track]
Intro · [.on]#Problem# · Definitions · Solutions · AI skills · Wrap-up

[.lead]
`list.stream().filter(p).findFirst()`, one call, match at element 500.

[cols="6,1,2",options="header",frame=none,grid=rows]
|===
| Origin | Objects | Bytes
| Stream source: `ReferencePipeline$Head`, spliterator | 2 | 80 B
| `filter` stage and its sink | 2 | 72 B
| terminal op: `FindSink`, `Optional` | 2 | 32 B
| caller lambda `Integer.valueOf(test)::equals` | 1 | 16 B
| `Integer` box, outside the cache | 1 | 16 B
| *Total per call* | *8* | *216 B*
|===

[.notes]
--
⏱ 13:00, stay about 45 s. Problem statement: 07:00 to 15:00 (8 min).

Class origins read from the JDK 25 bytecode with `javap`. `anyMatch` needs no filter stage, but its terminal op costs 64 B
(`MatchOp`, a sink-supplier lambda and the sink, all new on every call). `count` makes a new `ReduceOps$5` every call
although it holds no per-call state; caching it would save 16 B (inferred, not tested).
