Bytes per call are measured; rates and spikes are derived.

|===
| Element 500 | B per call | MB/s at 10 M calls/s | 10 s spike

[.notes]
--
⏱ 13:45, stay about 40 s. Problem statement: 07:00 to 15:00 (8 min).

Rate = bytes per call × calls per second; spike = rate × 10 s, the young-generation space the spike needs or the
collections it causes. GC runs per billion calls are not shown: at element 500 a 40 s measurement saw only 2 to 6 collections
(G1, 2 GB heap), too few to rank the three. Run 20261015-0406 gave 77, 141 and 199, run 20261017-1708 gave 154, 149 and 140.
`gc.alloc.rate` (MB/s) in JMH scales with how fast the benchmark runs; size heaps with bytes per call.
