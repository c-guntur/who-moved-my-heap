== Movers and Shakers

[.track]
Intro · [.on]#Problem# · Definitions · Solutions · AI skills · Wrap-up

image::diagram-problem-01-baseline-volatile.svg[Heap used over time: a baseline, a sawtooth of garbage and a spike,role=diagram]

* Headroom is what is left above the baseline for garbage.
* A spike multiplies the garbage rate while the baseline stays where it was.
* *Movers* focus on baseline memory.
* *Shakers* focus on volatile memory.
